Work

Concept websites

Everything below is self-directed concept work: complete dealership sites designed end to end, hero to footer, to show how a storefront can be built around real inventory instead of a stock template. The dealerships are invented and the vehicle photography belongs to the manufacturers — these are design studies, not client work, and they aren't affiliated with or endorsed by Ford, McLaren or Tesla. You don't have to take it on faith either: request a free 15-minute call and we'll build your homepage the same way, free, before you pay anything.

Concept 01

Meridian Ford — Franchise Dealer, Arizona

A full-site concept for a high-volume Ford franchise: inventory cards that carry the price and the spec on the card itself, a payment estimator that answers "what's my monthly?" before anyone calls, and an appointment picker instead of a contact form. Fifteen screens, hero to footer.

Concept 02

McLaren Mayfair — Supercar Retailer, London

The same thinking at the opposite end of the market: a low-volume retailer where fourteen cars have to carry the whole site. Restraint over volume — bigger photography, fewer words, and a specification treated as typography rather than a table.

Concept 03

Meridian Gallery — Tesla Specialist, Los Angeles

An independent EV gallery rather than a franchise: one badge, five models, and a buyer who already knows the product. The site leans on comparison and specification instead of persuasion, and switches between dark and light sections to separate showroom storytelling from the practical business of choosing a car. Nine screens.
