Work

Concept websites

This page covers site-design work specifically — there's no dealership portfolio to show yet, and nothing on this page is a fabricated client project. What follows are concept directions: full mockup redesigns built for real independent and family-run dealerships, using their actual branding, inventory photography, and the specific flaw found on their live site. None of these dealers commissioned this work or have endorsed it. For details on services, see Services.

Concept 01

North Wales Cars — Family-Run Dealer

A redesign concept for a real family-run used-car dealer in North Wales — warm, community-facing, built around their actual inventory and reviews. Fixes two flaws found on their live site: opening hours that mixed 12- and 24-hour formatting, and a generic, non-branded contact email.

Concept 02

Just Nice Clean Cars — Dealer & MOT Garage

A redesign concept for a real family-run dealer and MOT garage trading since 1991 — badge-row trust signals, real inventory, structured as garage-plus-dealer. Fixes the flaw on their live site: a vehicle search that showed "0 vehicles in stock" directly beneath a banner promising 30 cars.

Concept 03

PS Kraftwagen — Family Dealer, Germany

A redesign concept for a real founder-led German dealer — personal, founder-forward, muted charcoal and steel blue. Fixes the flaw on their live site: their own domain had zero vehicles listed for sale, working only as a "sell us your car" lead form while all real stock lived on third-party marketplaces.
